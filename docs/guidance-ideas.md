# Guidance ideas

The city is huge and has no UI, so the world itself must lead the player toward points of interest
(see "Guidance" in `docs/vision.md`). Wind and drifting pollen, as in *Ghost of Tsushima*, was judged
too obvious: the owner wants a real "aha" moment with the sound. Nothing here is implemented.

All of these fit the lore (the player is a machine listening to human souls, `docs/lore.md`).

1. **Harmony as a compass.** The city's low hum sounds slightly out of tune, with notes grinding
   against each other. Heading toward a point of interest, it slowly resolves into a clean consonant
   chord; it drifts apart again when the player turns away. The flower notes the player triggers are
   in the key of the distant chord, so their own melody starts to fit it.
2. **A choir that grows.** From far away: one very low sustained note, with a direction. Approaching,
   more voices join (fifth, third, octave), like a choir assembling. On arrival a whole chord opens
   and every soul around lights up at once. The aha: you were walking toward a song.
3. **The echo.** A key sends out a soft ping and the city answers: echoes bounce off walls, but one
   comes back later and clearer from the direction of an interest point, and the flowers on the way
   flash in sequence like a wave of light running toward it. Only a player who asks is guided.
4. **The melody trail.** Souls along a path each hold the next note of one melody, an old human
   song. Walking the path unfolds it, one note per flower, and it ends at the point of interest. The
   aha: the stray notes heard earlier were the start of a tune.
5. **Quiet visual cue (pairs with 1 or 2).** Flowers lean their petals slightly toward the nearest
   interest point, like sunflowers. Very subtle, found by chance.

Owner's choice: *to be decided.* Suggested: 1 and 2 together (harmony guides from afar, the choir
pays off on arrival), the echo later as an on-demand version.

Open question: what a point of interest is in the lore (where many souls gathered, the last place
humans lived, something the machines were building). Points of interest must be generated from the
seed.
