# Guidance ideas

The city is huge and has no UI, so the world itself must lead the player toward points of interest
(`docs/vision.md`, "Guidance"). Wind and drifting pollen, as in *Ghost of Tsushima*, was judged
too obvious: the owner wants a real "aha" moment with the sound. Nothing here is implemented.

All of these fit the lore (the player is the consciousness of a machine listening to human souls,
`docs/lore.md`).

**Owner's choice: the melodic gradient** ("it is cool"). The other ideas stay as options to enrich
it later (e.g. leaning flowers, as a subtle visual twin).

- **Melodic gradient** (from the brainstorm, `docs/game-ideas.md`). The flowers themselves guide.
  Far from a target their notes are sparse and unresolved; nearer, they grow more consonant and
  warmer and start forming a motif, which completes on arrival. Close to the harmony compass and
  the melody trail, but carried only by the souls. Never explained. The first target sits near the
  start with a strong gradient; later ones are subtler. A visual echo exists only as an
  accessibility option, off by default. In the story, the dead are leading the player toward the
  truth, and the faintest gradient of all leads to the unique rainbow biome.
- **Harmony compass.** The city's low hum sounds slightly out of tune, with notes grinding against
  each other. Heading toward a point of interest, it slowly resolves into a clean consonant chord;
  it drifts apart again when the player turns away. The flower notes the player triggers are in
  the key of the distant chord, so their own melody starts to fit it.
- **Growing choir.** From far away: one very low sustained note, with a direction. Approaching,
  more voices join (fifth, third, octave), like a choir assembling. On arrival a whole chord opens
  and every soul around lights up at once. The aha: you were walking toward a song.
- **Echo.** A key sends out a soft ping and the city answers: echoes bounce off walls, but one
  comes back later and clearer from the direction of an interest point, and the flowers on the way
  flash in sequence like a wave of light running toward it. Only a player who asks is guided.
- **Melody trail.** Souls along a path each hold the next note of one melody, an old human song.
  Walking the path unfolds it, one note per flower, and it ends at the point of interest. The aha:
  the stray notes heard earlier were the start of a tune.
- **Leaning flowers** (a quiet visual cue, pairs with the harmony compass or the growing choir).
  Flowers lean their petals slightly toward the nearest interest point, like sunflowers. Very
  subtle, found by chance.

Open question: what a point of interest is in the lore. The brainstorm answers with places that
hold human traces (a notice board in a courtyard dense with flowers, the foundation stone at the
bottom), craters of sunlight, the empty top, and the rainbow biome; see `docs/game-ideas.md`.
Points of interest must be generated from the seed.
